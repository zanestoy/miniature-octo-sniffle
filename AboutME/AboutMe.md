# About
## Mission Statement
Live, Learn, Grow.  I want to learn everything that I can, in many areas of my life.  I will never pass up an opportunity to learn.  One important step in the learning process is experience, live what you learn and learn what you live.  Ultimately, the learning process will make you grow.  I strive to live these principles, and apply them into all areas of my life.  I try to understand the problems I face by learning as much as I can about them.  I apply that knowledge, and try to understand the proper application of the knowledge I have learned.  I try to learn as an individual and as a team member.  Growth occurs as I gain experience.  I try grow a little each day, by gaining knowledge and experience.  The growth I strive for encompasses breadth and depth, which I feel is necessary for one to be balanced.
