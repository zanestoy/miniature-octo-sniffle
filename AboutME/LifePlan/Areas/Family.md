## 5. Assessment criteria

### Original Life Plan questions (page 11)

- How is your relationship with your wife?
- How is your relationship with your kids?
- How is your relationship with your family?
- How is your relationship with your friends?

### Proposed six-week reflection prompts

Use these to inform a brief narrative rather than answer every question each review:

- Where was I present, attentive, and dependable?
- Which relationship needs deliberate attention or repair?
- Did my calendar and decisions reflect the importance I assign to family?
- Which shared experiences or conversations mattered most?
- Where do I need to adjust expectations, boundaries, or commitments?

### Review record (stored in the six-week review, not here)

- **Score:** optional 1–10, based on my own definition of success.
- **Trend:** improving / steady / declining / unknown compared with the last review.
- **Mode:** Maintain / Improve / Restore.
- **Evidence:** one or two concrete observations.
- **Next action:** only if needed; a six-week outcome goes in the review document.

A score summarizes reflection; it is not a grade for other people or a measure of their behavior.
