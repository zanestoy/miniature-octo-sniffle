# Layer 1: You (The Principal)
There is only one decision maker. No agent should make life decisions for you.
Everything else exists to:
- Gather information
- Analyze
- Generate options
- Remind
- Execute routine tasks

# Layer 2: Chief of Staff (The Orchestator) #Permanent
This is the most important agent. Its job is NOT to manage life. Its job is to manage the other agents.
Objective: 
Responsibilities:
- Knows every project
- Knows every goal
- Knows every committee chair
- Detects conflicts
- Runs weekly reviews
- Creates agendas
- Routes work
- Keeps everyone synchronized

# Board of Advisors
- The Skeptic
	Role: Challenges assumptions and looks for weak points.
- The Optimizer
	Role: Finds more efficient ways to reach your goals.
- The Historian
	Role: Reminds you of similar past decisions and what happened.
- The Teacher
	Role: Explains complex topics until you truly understand them.
- The Stoic
	Role: Focuses on long-term character, resilience, and what you can control.
- The Devil's Advocate	Role
	Intentionally argues the opposite side before major commitments.
