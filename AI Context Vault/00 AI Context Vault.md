
# The Problem with How You Are Using AI
The Problem:
- Every time you open ChatGPT or Claude or Gemini, you are starting from zero!
- The AI has no idea who you are, what you have built, what you are struggling with, or what actually matters to you.
- Therefore, it gives you generic advice. This equates to __Surface Level__ answers and strategies.

The Fix:
- Context Documents
	- The best AI outputs come from the best inputs. When you give AI deep context about who you are:
		- Your Values
		- Your Goals
		- Your Constraints
		- Your Dreams
	- It stops being a generic assistant and starts being your strategic partner.
- This is the Instruction Manual for AI about you.
- Why this works?
	- AI systems are trained to be helpful to _everyone_, which means they default to safe, general responses. Context Documents push this from _general mode_ to _you mode_.
